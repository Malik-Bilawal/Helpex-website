// Our Story

Our Story & Brand Journey

At just sixteen, our founder Hasnain noticed something troubling: businesses were investing in technology but receiving only software — without guidance, training, or long-term partnership. That realization sparked The Helpex. Now 3+ years later — 5 products, 7 organizations, 88% client retention, and global collaborations. Proof that technology built with empathy works.

2023

Founded The Helpex — first clients onboarded

2024

Grew team, expanded service offerings

2025

5 products · 7 orgs · 88% retention

2026

Launching SaaS & global expansion
